How I work

Grow capacity

AI should give people time back for the work that needs them: clients, design, judgment, relationships. I measure success by what your team can do afterward.

Learn on real work

Training happens on your own tasks and your own data. People learn faster when the first thing they build is something they'll use on Monday.

Keep thinking

Good AI use makes people sharper. I teach when to lean on AI, when to check its work, and when to do the thinking yourself.

Evidence over hype

There's a lot of AI noise. I cite where claims come from, say how strong the evidence is, and tell you when nobody knows yet.

Who I work with

Business owners

Where does AI fit in your business, and where should you start? We map your workflows, pick the few worth changing, and plan how your team will adopt them.

Teams

Hands-on sessions on the work your team already does: call notes and summaries, project tracking, drafting, research. In person or remote, with practice between sessions.

Community groups

Sponsored sessions for schools, senior centers, and rec centers. Practical, confident AI use with good habits from the start. Interested in sponsoring one? Get in touch.

About

I'm Dave Wiseman. I help people and their data work together.

I've spent 12+ years turning operational data into tools people actually use, as a data scientist, an operations leader, and a designer of AI training. I've built machine learning models, so I know what the technology can and can't do.

At Guild, education coaches helped working adults decide whether going back to school made sense. We had a model that ranked who to reach first, but leads were still handed out by hand. I led a six-person team, most of whom didn't report to me, to rebuild how leads reached 100 coaches in Salesforce, starting with the coaches mocking up the system they wanted. Assignment became automatic, and each coach got a steady few hundred leads. That part, helping people change how they work, is usually the hard part. It's also the part I care about most.

I have a master's in analytics from Texas A&M and completed the Oxford AI Programme at Saïd Business School. I live in the Denver area and work in person and remotely.
